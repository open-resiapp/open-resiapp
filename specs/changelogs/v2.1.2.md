# Release v2.1.2

## New Features

- **Multi-kind communities** — Support for garden communities, garage buildings, streets, and custom hierarchies — not just HOAs.
- **Plugin/module system** — Install and toggle optional modules (intercom, camera, push notifications) from the admin UI.
- **Bulk QR self-registration** — Print a registration QR; residents scan and self-register, admin approves and assigns flats.
- **Dark mode** — Use the app in light, dark, or system-matched theme via header toggle.
- **Community marketplace** — Buy, sell, give away, or lend items between neighbors inside the building.
- **Community help posts** — Ask for or offer small neighborly favors (plant-watering, dog-walking, minor repairs).
- **Community events** — Organize neighbor gatherings (barbecues, clean-up days, holidays) with RSVP.
- **Neighbor directory** — Opt-in shared directory; choose what to share (phone, email, note, skills).
- **Per-entrance voting & posts** — Schedule votes or post notices targeting one entrance, not the whole building.
- **Czech localization & country setting** — Set a building's country to Czech Republic; UI and legal terminology switch to Czech.
- **Czech board model (výbor)** — Czech collective statutory body as alternative to predseda + rada.

## Improvements

- **Country-aware voting rules** — Voting engine applies SK or CZ legal rules based on the building's country.
- **Community email notifications** — Email alerts for post reactions, event reminders, and auto-expiration of stale posts.
- **External API hardening** — Rate-limiting, audit logging, and brute-force protection on the public API.
